# Background Music & Sound Effects 🎵

This directory contains the audio assets for the website:

| Filename | Purpose | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `birthday-music.mp3` | Background Music | Plays gently in the background once she unlocks the envelope. |
| `opening.mp3` | Opening Sound | Plays when she clicks "Open Your Surprise" on the envelope. |
| `celebration.mp3` | Celebration Sound | Plays when she clicks "Celebrate!" on the birthday cake. |
| `click.mp3` | Interactive Sound | Gentle acoustic chime when clicking interactive stars and buttons. |

## How to replace with your favorite song:
1. Choose an MP3 file (e.g. her favorite acoustic song or meaningful tune).
2. Rename it to `birthday-music.mp3` and place it here in `public/audio/`.
3. If an audio file is missing or fails to load, the site has a built-in **Web Audio API synthesizer fallback** so the experience always has music and sound without breaking!
